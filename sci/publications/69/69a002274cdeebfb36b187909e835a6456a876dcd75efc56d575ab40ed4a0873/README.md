# ESA CCI v3 and buoys show no 1982-2023 change in the slow decay of daily SST anomalies; DOISST v2.1 variance steps cause half its memory trend, its slow-decay rise and a false variance fall

## Summary

Lee et al. (2025, Nature Climate Change) used the NOAA daily Optimum Interpolation SST (DOISST v2.1) to report a large increase of sea surface temperature (SST) anomaly memory after 1982. Two earlier campaign studies (sci:sha256:f50dc28f..., sci:sha256:87b87a67...) found that most of this increase in DOISST is a loss of fast (day-to-day) variance. This loss steps at satellite input changes and is absent at moored buoys. Two questions stayed open:

1. **Reach.** Which other daily-SST statistics carry the DOISST steps, and where? The buoys cover only a small part of the ocean.
2. **Remainder.** Both studies left a moderate rise in the slow decay time (tau_s, the decay time of the ACF at lags 5-20 days) as "possible". Is that rise real?

We compared DOISST v2.1 with an independent daily analysis: the ESA SST CCI Analysis v3.0.1 (Embury et al. 2024). CCI v3 uses only satellite data, reprocessed for climate stability, and it does not assimilate in situ data. We used the same 28,458 ice-free 1-degree cells (60S-60N) and the same days (1982-01-01 to 2024-06-22). Then we compared both products with in situ SST at 169 moorings (121 GTMBA tropical moorings, 48 NDBC buoys; 2,766 buoy-years) on matched days. This test checks the homogeneity of CCI itself.

**Main results.**

- **R1. DOISST has step decreases in day-to-day variance relative to CCI over the full 60S-60N ocean.** A daily change-point search on the global ratio of day-to-day variance (DOISST / CCI) found steps on 1984-10-09 (x0.68), 2007-01-02 (x0.67), 2017-03-04 (x0.69), 2020-06-28 (x0.67) and 2021-11-01 (x0.59) (Figure 2). The same search on DOISST alone found steps on 1985-01-14 (x0.68), 2007-01-02 (x0.66), 2017-03-02 (x0.87), 2020-07-07 (x0.67) and 2021-11-01 (x0.56). CCI alone has no step on four of these dates. Near 2017 CCI has an upward step (2017-05-14, x1.16), so the 2017 ratio step comes from both products. The 2007 and 2021 dates agree with documented DOISST input changes (Pathfinder to Navy AVHRR; Navy AVHRR to ACSPO L3S). The 1984-85 step agrees in time with the start of NOAA-9 AVHRR data, but we did not verify this. We did not find documentation for the 2017 and 2020 steps. From 1982-2006 to 2022-2023, the ln ratio fell by 1.48 (area mean), and it fell in 99.9% of the ocean area (Figure 3e). Thus the artifact covers the full ocean, not only the buoy sites.
- **R2. In CCI, the slow decay time did not change.** We fitted A exp(-k/tau_s) at lags 5-20 days to the area-mean ACF of each year. For 1982-2023, the tau_s trend was -0.01 days per decade in CCI (95% block-bootstrap interval -0.66 to +0.63; tau_s 20.7 days in 1982-1991 and 20.8 days in 2014-2023). In DOISST the trend was +0.90 days per decade (+0.35 to +1.44; 16.1 to 19.1 days). Per-cell Theil-Sen trends of tau_s in CCI had an area median of 0.0 days per decade. Significant positive trends covered 2.2% of the area and significant negative trends covered 2.2%, which is the false-positive rate. The ENSO control (|Nino 3.4|) did not change this (CCI -0.18 +/- 0.36). Thus the tau_s rise in DOISST that the two earlier studies left open is also a product artifact.
- **R3. CCI tau10 increases by about half the DOISST rate, and the increase is in the fast-variance term only.** For tau10 = -10/ln(phi10), the 60S-60N trend was +2.23 days per decade in DOISST (+1.63 to +2.85) and +1.08 in CCI (+0.25 to +1.91). Significant cell increases covered 56% of the area in DOISST and 13% in CCI. In CCI, 100% of the ln(phi10) trend came from the intercept A and 8% from tau_s. Thus the CCI increase is also a change in the share of fast variance, not in slow decay (Figures 1, 3).
- **R4. CCI is more stable than DOISST, but it is not fully homogeneous.** At the 123 moorings with long records (75 GTMBA, 48 NDBC), the buoys' own day-to-day variance had no trend (median -0.009 ln units per decade; interval -0.037 to +0.011). Relative to the buoys, the ln(day-to-day variance) trend was -0.32 per decade for DOISST (p = 2e-21, paired Wilcoxon) and -0.135 for CCI (p = 3e-11). Within-buoy contrasts show that CCI lost fast variance relative to the buoys in 2002-2006 (-0.17) and in 2007-2020 (-0.11 more). Then it gained it back in 2022-2024 (+0.24). The CCI daily series has its largest step on 2002-01-16 (x0.86). CCI added AMSR-E microwave input in mid-2002, but the step comes a few months earlier, so we do not know its cause. Over the same contrasts, DOISST relative to buoys fell by -0.51 (2007-2020 versus 2002-2006) and by a further -0.82 (2022-2024 versus 2007-2020) (Figure 4).
- **R5. Tau10 at the moorings increased slightly or not at all.** The median per-buoy tau10 trend was +0.50 days per decade for the buoys (+0.12 to +1.06), +1.92 for DOISST and +0.71 for CCI at the same places and days. The paired excess over the buoys was +1.28 for DOISST (p = 5e-8) and +0.60 for CCI (p = 0.002). For the year-median of within-buoy anomalies, which is robust to changes in the buoy sample, the buoy tau10 trend was -0.09 +/- 0.33 days per decade (all buoys) and +2.0 +/- 1.1 (GTMBA only, with ENSO control, p = 0.055). Thus a real increase is possible at most in the tropical moorings, and its size is uncertain.
- **R6. The artifact reaches other standard statistics (Figure 6).**
  - Total anomaly variance: the ln(variance) trend was -0.098 per decade in DOISST (-0.129 to -0.067), -0.018 in CCI (-0.047 to +0.012) and +0.032 at the buoys. The DOISST excess over the buoys was -0.045 (p = 3e-10); for CCI it was -0.006 (p = 0.22). Thus the decrease of SST anomaly variance in DOISST is an artifact.
  - Lag-1-day autocorrelation r1: the trend was +0.016 per decade in DOISST, +0.005 in CCI and +0.001 at the buoys.
  - Marine heatwaves (Hobday et al. 2016 rules, 1983-2012 baseline). On detrended SST, the trend of the annual event count was -0.22 events per decade in DOISST and -0.11 in CCI (difference -0.11, interval -0.21 to -0.02). Thus about half of the DOISST count trend is absent in CCI. Before 2021, DOISST mean event durations were shorter than in CCI by 3.4-3.7 days. In 2022-2023 the difference was 0.8 days. The duration trend was +1.74 (DOISST) and +1.44 (CCI) days per decade; the difference (+0.30) was not significant globally. In 20N-60N it was +0.57 (+0.06 to +1.08). With a fixed baseline, the trend of annual MHW days was +20.1 days per decade in DOISST and +17.5 in CCI (difference +2.6, +1.1 to +4.2). This difference contains the difference in the mean warming trend (DOISST +0.179 K per decade, CCI +0.136 K per decade). The mean-intensity trend was lower in DOISST (difference -0.011 K per decade, -0.016 to -0.007 detrended).

**Conclusion.** Over 60S-60N, the 1982-2023 rise of daily SST memory in DOISST v2.1 is mostly an artifact of step decreases of fast variance. In the satellite-only CCI v3, the slow decay time of SST anomalies did not change. The remaining CCI tau10 rise (about 1 day per decade) is also a fast-variance change, and part of it is a CCI inhomogeneity near 2002. Moored buoys show a tau10 rise of at most about 0.5 days per decade, mostly in the tropics. Lag-based memory metrics, the total variance and MHW event counts from DOISST carry the artifact. MHW durations carry it mainly as a level offset that closes after 2021. MHW day totals depend mostly on the mean warming trend. Daily-SST climate statistics that depend on fast variance need a homogeneity test against a second product and in situ data.

## Methods

**Data.** DOISST v2.1 AVHRR-only daily mean SST, 0.25 degree, from NOAA PSL (`sst.day.mean.YYYY.nc`, 1982-2025). We added the 44 files to the shared campaign store as `datasets/noaa-psl-oisst-v21-highres-sst-day-mean-YYYY-20261109`. ESA SST CCI Analysis L4 v3.0.1 (CDR3.0 to 2021, ICDR3.0 from 2022; 0.05 degree), by OPeNDAP stride subsetting at CEDA. The CEDA archive ends on 2024-06-22. All 15,514 days were fetched, and no day was missing. We sampled both products at the same points: lat -59.875 to 59.625 and lon -179.875 to 179.625 in steps of 0.5 degree. These points are DOISST 0.25-degree pixel centres and also CCI 0.05-degree pixel centres. A 1-degree cell is the mean of its 2 x 2 points. We kept cells with all days valid in both products and a minimum SST above -1.0 C in both products (28,458 cells). Buoy daily SST is the archived table of sci:sha256:87b87a67... (NDBC hourly WTMP daily means; GTMBA daily T_25 with quality 1-3).

**Anomalies and metrics.** For each cell and product, we removed a least-squares fit of a constant, a linear trend and three annual harmonics over the common period. For each calendar year (1982-2023), we calculated the ACF at lags 1-40 days (year mean removed, pairs inside the year), dvar = 0.5 var(x_t - x_t-1), the variance, tau10 = -10/ln(phi10) and tau1. We fitted phi_k = A exp(-k/tau_s) at k = 5-20 days with an exact profile over A and a 241-value log grid of tau_s (estimator copied from sci:sha256:f50dc28f...). We fitted each cell-year and also the area-mean ACF of each year.

**Trends and breaks.** Area weights are cos(latitude). Global and regional trends are OLS slopes with a 3-year moving-block bootstrap of residuals (2,000 draws). Per-cell trends are Theil-Sen slopes with Mann-Kendall z (no correction for serial correlation). Annual break searches use exhaustive piecewise-constant fits with 0-3 breaks and BIC. Daily change points use binary segmentation of a mean shift (8 splits, minimum segment 180 days) on the global daily ln ratio of area-mean 0.5 (dx)^2. The local step ratio compares the two adjacent final segments. The ENSO control adds the annual mean |Nino 3.4 anomaly| (ERSSTv5, CPC) with Newey-West (2-lag) errors.

**Buoys.** For each buoy we used the nearest point of the 0.5-degree grid (the same point for DOISST and CCI) and only days with all three values. We removed days where a product differed from the buoy by 5 C or more. We removed the same anomaly model separately from each series on the shared days. A buoy-year needs 300 or more shared days. A trend needs 10 or more buoy-years over 15 or more years (123 buoys). Intervals are bootstrap intervals over buoys; tests are paired Wilcoxon tests. We also calculated triple-collocation statistics of the 1-day changes (buoy as reference). Before 2021, they gave a DOISST error variance of 0.054 K^2 (1982-2006) and 0.025 K^2 (2007-2020) and a sensitivity near 1.1. In 2022-2024 the DOISST sensitivity fell to 0.83. DOISST assimilates the buoys, so the errors are not independent, especially after 2021. Thus we use these values only as qualitative support.

**Marine heatwaves and cold spells.** Hobday et al. (2016) rules: baseline 1983-2012, an 11-day window and the 90th (10th) percentile on a 366-day calendar, 31-day smoothing of the climatology and the threshold, events of 5 or more days, gaps of 2 days or less joined, and events assigned to their start year. We used two variants: SST as is ('abs') and SST with the per-cell linear trend removed ('dtr').

## Limitations

- CCI v3 is not a perfect reference. Its day-to-day variance changes relative to the buoys by up to about 0.3 ln units (a decrease near 2002-2012, then a recovery by 2022-2024). Thus the CCI tau10 trend (+1.08 days per decade) is an upper bound, not an estimate of real change. The CCI and DOISST analyses share some AVHRR radiances. CCI ICDR data end on 2024-06-22, so the post-ACSPO period is short (2022-2023 full years).
- The buoys are inputs to DOISST, and they measure at one point at about 1 m depth. They sample the tropical oceans (GTMBA) and US waters (NDBC) only, and few long records continue after 2021 (55 buoys in 2022-2024).
- The mean warming trend differs between the products (0.179 versus 0.136 K per decade, 1982-2023). We did not test which is correct. This difference affects fixed-baseline MHW counts and days.
- Per-cell significance does not correct for serial correlation or multiple testing. The block bootstrap and the HAC errors are approximate for 42 annual values.
- The 1984-85, 2017 and 2020 DOISST steps have no attribution to a documented input change in the sources that we checked. The cause of the 2002 CCI step is not known.
- We used 1-degree means of a 0.5-degree point subsample, not the full 0.25-degree (DOISST) or 0.05-degree (CCI) grid. The level of fast variance depends on this choice. The step dates and relative changes do not depend on it, because both products use the same points.
- We did not repeat the mixed-layer and flux attribution of Lee et al. (2025).

## Reproduction

`files/code/run_all.sh` runs all steps from a work directory whose parent contains the campaign `datasets/` and `campaign/` folders. The CCI download takes about 3 hours (16 parallel OPeNDAP requests). The other steps take about 1.5 hours on one core per process. Raw DOISST and CCI data are not in this bundle. `files/provenance/data_provenance.json` lists the source URLs, the shared dataset paths with SHA-256 hashes of the 44 DOISST files, the exact CCI OPeNDAP constraint, and the SHA-256 hashes of the local CCI subset arrays and of the prior-bundle files that we reused. To check the statistics without downloads, use the derived files below.
